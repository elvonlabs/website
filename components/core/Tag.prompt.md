**Tag** — soft uppercase pill for research domains and labels. Rotate the color family to add warmth and rhythm.

```jsx
<Tag>AI</Tag>
<Tag variant="coral">Healthcare</Tag>
<Tag variant="mint">Foundational CS</Tag>
```

Variants: `indigo` (default), `coral`, `sun`, `mint`, `neutral`. Group with `display:flex; gap:8px`.
