**StatCard** — a big display figure over a muted label, on a soft pastel tile. For citation counts, outcomes, durations. Rotate `tone` across a row for rhythm.

```jsx
<StatCard value="634" label="Citations" tone="lavender" />
<StatCard value="14" label="Weeks" tone="peach" />
<StatCard value="1:1" label="Mentorship" tone="mint" />
```

Tones: `lavender`, `peach`, `butter`, `mint`, `plain`.
